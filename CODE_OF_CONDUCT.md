# Code of Conduct

ModFerry follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want ModFerry to be a friendly place for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to modding or to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on the problem, not the person.
- Assume good intentions and ask before you judge.
- Respect that maintainers work on this in their free time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information (including API tokens) without permission
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently block contributors.

Report problems privately to the maintainer via a [private security advisory](https://github.com/CptGummiball/ModFerry/security/advisories/new) or a direct message on GitHub. All reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, the wiki) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
